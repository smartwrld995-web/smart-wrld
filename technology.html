01. ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE

WHAT IS AI?

Artificial Intelligence, commonly called AI, is technology that allows computer systems to perform tasks that normally require human intelligence.

HOW DOES IT WORK?

Many AI systems learn patterns from examples and information called data. They use those patterns to make predictions or produce results.

REAL-LIFE EXAMPLES

Voice assistants, language translation, image recognition and recommendation systems are examples of AI applications.

02. ROBOTICS

WHAT IS ROBOTICS?

Robotics is a field of engineering and technology focused on designing, building and operating robots.

IMPORTANT PARTS OF A ROBOT

A robot may contain sensors, motors, a controller and a power source. These parts work together to help it perform tasks.

WHERE ARE ROBOTS USED?

Robots are used in factories, scientific research, healthcare, space exploration and other areas.

03. COMPUTING

WHAT IS COMPUTING?

Computing involves using computers and computational systems to process information and solve problems.

HARDWARE AND SOFTWARE

Hardware refers to the physical parts of a computer, such as the keyboard, screen and processor. Software refers to the programs that run on it.

WHY IS COMPUTING IMPORTANT?

Computing helps people communicate, learn, design products, analyze information and develop new technologies.
